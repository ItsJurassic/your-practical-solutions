Here’s what to expect after you place an order. Most orders arrive within 5–12 business days, and every order gets a tracking number.

Order processing

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding U.S. holidays). Orders placed after 2:00 PM Central Time or on weekends start processing the next business day. You’ll receive an order confirmation email right away.

Typical delivery times

DestinationProcessingTransit (after shipping)Typical total
Contiguous United States1–3 business days4–9 business days5–12 business days
Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories1–3 business days7–14 business days8–17 business days

These are estimates, not guarantees. Peak seasons, weather, customs checks, and carrier delays can add time. Because some items ship directly from our suppliers, an order with several items may arrive in separate packages on different days.

Shipping costs

Shipping costs (or free-shipping offers, when available) are shown at checkout before you pay.

Tracking your order

As soon as your order ships, we email you a tracking number and link. Tracking can take 24–72 hours to show its first update. You can also check your order status from the link in your confirmation email. If tracking hasn’t updated for 10 business days, contact us.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us right away — we can update it if the order hasn’t shipped yet. We’re not responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address provided at checkout, but we’ll always try to help.

Damaged, late, or lost packages

See our Return & Refund Policy — damaged items are replaced or refunded with photo proof, and lost packages are replaced or refunded after we confirm with the carrier.

International orders

We currently ship within the United States only.

Questions? Email contact@yourpracticalsolutions.com — a real person on our team will reply, usually within 1–2 business days.
