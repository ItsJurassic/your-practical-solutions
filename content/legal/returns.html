How to start a return

  1. Email us at contact@yourpracticalsolutions.com within 30 days of delivery, with the subject “Return request.”
  2. Include your order number, the item(s), the reason, and — for damaged, defective, or wrong items — clear photos of the item, the problem, and the shipping label/packaging.
  3. We reply within 1–2 business days with your options and, if a return is needed, the return address and instructions.
  4. Please don’t send items back without our instructions — returns to the wrong address (for example, the address on the package) can’t be matched to your order.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged, doesn’t work as described, or isn’t what you ordered, we’ll send a replacement or give you a full refund — your choice where stock allows — at no cost to you. Photo proof is all we usually need; in most cases you won’t have to ship the item back. If we do need it back, we’ll cover the return shipping.

Please report problems as soon as possible, ideally within 7 days of delivery, and always within 30 days.

Change-of-mind returns

If you simply changed your mind, we’ll do our best to help. Change-of-mind returns are handled case by case together with our supplier:

  • The item must be unused, in its original condition and packaging, with all parts and accessories.
  • The customer pays the return shipping cost. Some returns go back to our supplier’s warehouse, which may be outside the U.S.; we’ll tell you the address and expected cost before you decide. We recommend a tracked shipping method.
  • Original shipping charges are not refundable.
  • Once the supplier confirms receipt and condition, we refund the item price to your original payment method.

Non-returnable items

Unless they arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect, we can’t accept returns of:

  • Personal-care, hygiene, and intimate items that have been opened or used;
  • Consumables (e.g., cleaning products, food, pet treats) that have been opened;
  • Custom, personalized, or made-to-order items;
  • Items marked “Final sale” or clearance;
  • Gift cards;
  • Items damaged by misuse, improper installation, or normal wear and tear;
  • Items returned more than 30 days after delivery.

Refunds and timeline

  • Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 business days of approval (for damaged/defective/wrong items) or of the supplier confirming receipt (for change-of-mind returns).
  • Your bank or card issuer may take another 5–10 business days to post the credit.
  • We email you as soon as the refund is issued. If you haven’t seen it after 15 business days, contact us and we’ll follow up.
  • Exchanges: the fastest way to get a different item is to request a refund and place a new order.

Order cancellations

You can cancel for a full refund any time before your order ships. Email us as soon as possible with your order number — orders are often sent to our suppliers within 24 hours. If the order has already shipped, we can’t cancel it, but you can return it under this policy.

Late or lost packages

If your tracking hasn’t updated for 10 business days, or your order hasn’t arrived within 30 days of shipping, contact us. We’ll investigate with the carrier and, if the package is lost, send a replacement or issue a full refund.
