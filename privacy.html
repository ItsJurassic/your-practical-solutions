This Privacy Policy explains how Your Practical Solutions (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit yourpracticalsolutions.com (the “Site”), browse our catalog, or buy from our online store. If you have questions, email contact@yourpracticalsolutions.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you place an order or contact us, we receive your name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number (if provided), order details, and the content of your messages.

Payment information. Payments are processed by Shopify and its payment partners. We never see or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting providers and our store platform automatically receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring URLs. Our store platform may also use cookies (see Cookies and analytics).

How we use information

  • To process, fulfill, ship, and support your orders, including returns and refunds.
  • To communicate with you about your order, respond to questions, and provide customer service.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the Site and store secure.
  • To understand how the Site is used and to improve our products and services.
  • To send marketing emails only if you opt in; you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Shopify checkout

Our store, cart, and checkout are powered by Shopify. When you click a product or “Shop” link, you are taken to our Shopify store, and the information you enter there is collected and processed by Shopify on our behalf. Shopify’s handling of your data is described in the Shopify Privacy Policy.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business:

  • Shopify — online store, checkout, payments, and order management.
  • Suppliers and fulfillment partners — we work with third-party suppliers who ship products directly to you. They receive the information needed to fulfill your order (name, shipping address, phone if provided, and items ordered).
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your order and provide tracking.
  • Supabase — a cloud database we use for our internal team tools (such as sales reporting and our team chat) and to deliver our product catalog to this Site. Order data synchronized from Shopify for reporting is stored there with restricted access.
  • Website hosting — this Site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; these providers may log technical data such as IP addresses.
  • Legal and safety — when required by law, or to protect our rights, customers, or the public.

Cookies and analytics

This Site does not set its own advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently run third-party analytics on it. Our Shopify store uses cookies that are necessary for the cart and checkout to work, and may use analytics cookies to measure store performance, as described in Shopify’s cookie notice. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking necessary cookies may prevent checkout from working.

If we add analytics or advertising tools to this Site in the future, we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent first.

Your privacy rights (CCPA / GDPR)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions (for example, records we must keep for tax purposes);
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information — we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • Data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

California residents: we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Residents of the EU/UK: we process your data to perform our contract with you (fulfilling orders), to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in running and securing our store; your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

To make a request, email contact@yourpracticalsolutions.com with the subject “Privacy request.” We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally 30–45 days). You may use an authorized agent where the law allows.

Data retention

We keep order and transaction records for as long as needed to fulfill orders, handle returns and warranty questions, and meet tax, accounting, and legal requirements — generally up to seven (7) years after the order. Customer-service emails are kept for up to three (3) years. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so unless we are legally required to keep it.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards — such as encrypted connections and access controls — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our Site and store are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
